隐私政策

随行译 · Travel Translator
更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-01

随行译由个人开发者提供，联系邮箱：shifeng199307@gmail.com。

1. 本地处理

准备好所需的离线语言资源后，语音识别、翻译与语音合成在设备本地完成。App 不提供云端识别、云端翻译或云端播报兜底，不向开发者服务器上传原始音频、原文或译文。

2. 麦克风与后台使用

只有在你主动开始翻译或回应并授予权限后，App 才会使用麦克风。翻译运行时可能在锁屏或后台继续收音和播报，你可以在 App 内暂停或在系统设置撤销麦克风权限。正常翻译流程不把原始录音保存为长期音频文件；用于识别的短时音频缓冲会循环释放。

3. 本地字幕与偏好

原文、译文和相关语言信息保存在 App 的本地字幕数据库中，便于回看。语言、音色及设备偏好保存在本机。你可以在历史记录中删除单个会话或确认后删除全部会话；删除 App 也会移除其本地数据。设备备份是否包含 App 数据由 iOS 和你的备份设置决定；清空本机字幕不代表已有系统备份同步删除。

4. 诊断与第三方

App 可能在本机保留有限的耗时、模型加载和错误诊断信息，用于性能排查；这些信息不会由 App 自动发送给开发者。当前版本不接入广告、第三方统计、跨 App 跟踪、账号或支付服务。开发版工具及系统诊断不属于云端翻译功能。

5. 语言资源与系统服务

Apple 翻译语言包及部分系统语音资源需要预先通过系统下载。首次启动及缺少所需翻译包时，App 会请求 Apple 系统下载确认；请联网并在系统弹窗中同意，下载完成后再离线使用。下载、App 安装与更新、联系支持以及打开外部网页需要网络，相关系统服务由 Apple 或所打开服务的提供者按其政策处理。App 的离线核心功能不要求持续联网。

6. 联系支持

主动通过邮件联系支持时，你选择发送的邮件内容会经过所用邮件服务。请勿附上不必要的个人信息或他人的私密对话。隐私问题请发送至 shifeng199307@gmail.com。

7. 使用提醒

请在获得适当同意的情况下使用麦克风处理他人讲话。翻译可能出错，不宜作为医疗、法律或其他重大决定的唯一依据。

政策更新后会在 App 和公开隐私页面提供最新版本。

Privacy Policy

Travel Translator is provided by an independent developer. Contact: shifeng199307@gmail.com.

1. On-device processing

Once the required offline language resources are prepared, speech recognition, translation and speech synthesis run on your device. The app offers no cloud recognition, translation or speech fallback and does not upload original audio, source text or translations to a developer server.

2. Microphone and background use

The app uses the microphone only after you start translation or a reply and grant permission. While translation is running, audio input and playback may continue in the background or with the screen locked. You can pause in the app or revoke microphone permission in system settings. Normal translation does not save original recordings as long-term audio files; short audio buffers used for recognition are continually released.

3. Local captions and preferences

Source text, translations and related language information are stored in the app's local caption database for review. Language, voice and device preferences are stored on your device. You can delete individual sessions or all caption history in History. Deleting the app also removes its local data. Whether device backups include app data depends on iOS and your backup settings. Clearing local captions does not delete copies in existing system backups.

4. Diagnostics and third parties

The app may retain limited timing, model-loading and error diagnostics locally to investigate performance. The app does not automatically send this information to the developer. This version includes no advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, account or payment services. Development tools and system diagnostics are not cloud translation features.

5. Language resources and system services

Apple translation packs and some system voice resources require an initial system download. On first launch and when required Apple translation packs are missing, the app requests download consent through Apple’s system sheet. Stay online and approve that sheet to download the resources before offline use. Downloads, app installation and updates, contacting support and opening external websites require internet access. Apple or the provider of the service you open handles those services under its own policies. The app's core offline features do not require a continuous internet connection. The interface follows your iPhone language; this does not change the selected translation direction.

6. Contacting support

If you choose to contact support by email, the content you send passes through your email service. Do not include unnecessary personal information or other people's private conversations. Send privacy questions to shifeng199307@gmail.com.

7. Usage reminder

Obtain appropriate consent before using the microphone to process other people's speech. Translations may be wrong and should not be the sole basis for medical, legal or other important decisions.

Updated versions of this policy will be available in the app and on the public privacy page.
