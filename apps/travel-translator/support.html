技术支持

随行译 · Travel Translator
更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-01

出发前准备

在设置的“离线语言”中选择对方语言和己方语言。首次启动及缺少所需 Apple 翻译包时，App 会请求 Apple 系统下载确认；请联网并在系统弹窗中同意，完成首次资源下载后再离线使用。默认对方英语、己方简体中文。当前语言组合的双向翻译包实际安装完成前，开始按钮保持灰色；点击会提示等待。语言名称与 Apple 翻译的地区语言包一致，包括葡萄牙语（巴西）、西班牙语（西班牙）及普通话的简繁体；阿拉伯语不附国家。

同声传译

选择对方语言与己方语言，点开始。手机持续收音，显示原文与译文；连接耳机时播报译文，没有耳机时只显示字幕。可以向上滚动查看历史，回到底部跟随最新内容。

对话

点击开始后大家轮流讲话。对方讲话译成你的语言；己方讲话译成对方语言，通过手机扬声器播放。默认对方英语、己方中文。

锁屏与声音

翻译开启后支持锁屏继续运行，请先授权麦克风并在真机验证耳机连接。千问在前台本地生成声音；锁屏或内存不足等情况下会使用系统离线声音。持续翻译会耗电，实际表现随设备、噪声和资源准备情况变化。

语速和音量

译文积压时可保持音高加速至最高 1.5 倍。对话模式也会播报“hello”“谢谢”等单词或短回复；持续聆听仍会抑制极短片段。系统媒体音量请用实体音量键或控制中心调整，App 内没有媒体音量滑块。

会话与历史记录

顶部“＋”新建当前模式的会话；重启 App、切换模式或暂停后继续会保留当前会话。下拉字幕列表可每次加载更早的100条字幕。顶部历史按钮打开两种模式共用的“历史记录”，带有模式标签；点击查看，左滑可删除单个会话，也可确认后全部删除。 实际修改任何设置都会停止并保存两种模式，为两种模式各建新会话；仅打开设置、点击“完成”或试听不算修改。

常见问题

没有译文：确认两种翻译语言包均已下载且系统支持该方向，先联网准备资源后再离线使用。 没有声音：同声传译需要连接耳机；在设置选择输出设备，使用“试听”检查。对话回应译文使用手机扬声器。 收音不清：优先用 iPhone 麦克风，靠近说话人，减少风声与多人重叠讲话。 语音效果：可在播报模型中选择千问或系统离线播报，选择声音后试听。

Help and Support

Before you travel

In Settings > Offline Languages, choose their language and your language. On first launch and when required Apple Translation packs are missing, the app requests Apple’s system download consent. Stay online and approve the system sheet; resources must be downloaded once before offline use. Defaults are English for the other person and Simplified Chinese for you. Start remains gray until the selected pair is installed in both directions; tapping it shows a waiting message. Language names follow Apple Translate packs, including Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Spain), and Mandarin Chinese scripts; Arabic has no country label.

Simultaneous interpretation

Choose their language and your language, then tap Start. The phone listens continuously and displays original text and translations. With headphones connected, translations play through them; without headphones, only captions appear. Scroll up to review previous captions and return to the bottom to follow the latest.

Conversation

Tap Start and take turns speaking. The other person's speech is translated into your language. Your speech is translated into their language and played through the phone speaker. Defaults are English for the other person and Chinese for you.

Screen lock and voices

Translation can continue with the screen locked. Grant microphone permission and check your headphone connection on the device first. Qwen generates speech locally in the foreground. With the screen locked or memory low, the app uses a system offline voice. Continuous translation uses battery, and performance varies with your device, noise and prepared resources.

Speech rate and volume

Queued speech can accelerate up to 1.5× without changing pitch. Conversation also speaks single-word replies such as “hello” and “谢谢”; continuous listening still suppresses very short fragments. Use the physical volume buttons or Control Center for system media volume. There is no media volume slider in the app.

Sessions and history

Tap + at the top to start a new session in the current mode. Restarting the app, switching modes or continuing after a pause preserves the current session. Pull down the caption list to load the previous 100 captions. The History button opens a combined history for both modes, with mode labels. Tap a session to view it, swipe left to delete one, or confirm Delete All to delete every session. Any actual settings edit stops and saves both modes and creates a fresh session for each. Merely opening Settings, tapping Done or previewing a voice does not count as an edit.

Common questions

No translation: Check that both translation languages are downloaded and the system supports the chosen direction. Prepare the resources online before using them offline. No sound: Simultaneous interpretation requires headphones. Choose an output device in Settings and use Preview to check it. Your translated replies in Conversation play through the phone speaker. Unclear input: Prefer the iPhone microphone, move closer to the speaker and reduce wind and overlapping speech. Voice quality: Choose Qwen or system offline speech under Speech Model, select a voice and preview it.
